# the gentlemans lounge barbershop digital

Website Building Prompt:

Create a modern, clean, and mobile-friendly website for a barbershop called Karibu BarberShop, located in Seattle, Washington.

Business Details:

 Name: Karibu BarberShop

 Address: 3855 Rainier Ave S, Seattle, WA 98118

 Hours:

 Monday–Saturday: 9 AM – 8 PM

 Sunday: Closed

 Status: Open daily except Sunday phone number: 206-407-4147

Style & Branding:

 Theme: Sleek, urban, and professional barbershop vibe

 Colors: Black, gold, white, and dark wood tones

 Fonts: Bold, masculine, easy-to-read typography

 Include high-quality images of fades, tapers, and clean cuts (especially similar to the provided images)

Pages to Include:

1. Home Page

 Hero section with a strong headline like:
“Sharp Cuts. Clean Style. Seattle’s Go-To Barbershop.”

 Call-to-action button: “Book Now”

 Showcase haircut styles (fade, taper, lineup)

 Brief intro about the shop

2. About Page

 Story of the barbershop

 Emphasize professionalism, precision, and customer experience

3. Services Page

 List services such as:

 Haircuts

 Fades

 Beard trims

 Lineups

 Include pricing (use placeholder pricing if not provided)

4. Gallery

 Display haircut images (like fades and tapers from the example)

 Grid layout, clean and modern

5. Booking Page

 Online booking form

 Fields: Name, Phone, Service, Date/Time

 Add option for walk-ins vs appointments

6. Contact Page

 Address with embedded map

 Phone number placeholder

 Business hours clearly listed

Features:

 Mobile responsive design

 Sticky navigation bar

 Smooth scrolling

 Google Maps integration

 Social media links (Instagram preferred for barbershop content)

Tone:

 Professional but welcoming

 Confident and stylish

 Focus on quality and precision
